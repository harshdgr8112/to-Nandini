One last little thing

I kept something for you.

Aur bs ye thi meri thodi si koshish to make my special person's day special, aka Nandini Janmotsav.

Kaisa laga? I know thik hi hoga, could have been better, but chhodo wo sab, aapka din hai, aapki hi baatein hongi bas.

I know apan aaj saath nahi hai, but I know you are with me in every breath I take, every word I speak, every thought I process. And someday, itna difficult nahi hoga apna saath hona. Tab tak aise hi birthday wish le lo.

You deserve more, you deserve everything. I will make sure you get it, and I will not rest until you do.

Whenever the day is hard, whenever you are not able to think straight, always remember: I will always be there for you, standing by your side, holding your hand.

We will face everything together, no matter what — sadness, happiness, tumhara gussa, sab mein.

Coz you are my pretty little baby. I adore you, I care for you, I yearn for you. You are my kuchu puchu, my chotu sa bacha, my kaju baby.

You are all I need. I am so in love with you.

You are pretty, beautiful, hot — the piece that had been missing from my life all along.

Staying away from you is the hardest thing I have had to do. I didn't believe in soulmates until I met you.

The best thing about you is that you just get me; while others judge, you understand. This makes you the most wonderful person.

Maybe you don't notice, but every day I try to do the same: to find ways to make you feel good, safe, beautiful, and truly loved. I don't know if I always get it right, but those will forever be my intentions with you.

I hope this whole thing has made you smile even a little. If it did, bas main bhi khush fir.

Bas zyada tumhara time na lete hue, isko yahi khatam karte hai.

Main chahe gussa karu, ya baat na karu, ya rou, ya gaau, ya nachu, ya jo karu... always remember, I love you. Always and forever. Nothing will change.

Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️
